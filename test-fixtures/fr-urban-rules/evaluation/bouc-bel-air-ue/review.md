Validated by independent cross-check (a second model, Fable 5.2, that did not see this truth) reconciled by Claude Code; not validated by the owner on 2026-11-20.

Scope: The kinds the buildable area uses. Not covered (never counted as an extra): the floor-area caps and thresholds of article UE1 (50 m² for the existing dwelling, 200 m² in total per dwelling plot, 150 m² for a craft shop in UEa and UEb, 400 m² and 4 000 m² of a pôle de vie in UEp: floor_area_max), the distance of 30 m from an annex or a pool to the dwelling, the pitch of roofs (2 to 35 %), the alignment on the gabarit line of UEc (article 4.1.2, a sentence with no figure), the technical-need exception of 4.5.3, fences (2 m, 1.20 m), parking (1 place per 50 m² or 30 m²), stormwater figures; the figure coverage still watches them.
