## Goal

A person adds a lotissement, hameau, ZAC, SPR, copropriété or OAP PDF to the project (platform 122.0), chooses **which pages** to read, is told **before anything is sent** what it will cost in AI budget, and gets the rules it contains as **to-confirm** items marked with their source (document name, kind, page). A text PDF is read from its text; a **scan is read by vision**: Sonnet transcribes the chosen pages, then the PLU reading's own extraction and verification run on the transcript. Nothing read from an uploaded document is ever applied automatically, shared, or kept on the server.

## Baseline (verified against the source, 2026-11-21)

**The AI service (platform `apps/ai-service`, contract 1.13.0)**

- `POST /api/ai/extract` takes **text only**. Its request has a closed field list (`providerId`, `model`, `instructions`, `text`, `itemSchema`, `maxItems`, `maxTokens`; any other field is refused) and always calls the model with `images: []` (`structured/extract.ts`). **A scan cannot be sent through it.** True.
- `POST /api/ai/chat` **relays images** to the provider (`images: [{ mediaType, data }]`, validated in `ai-service.ts`), returns `finishReason` (`'length'` = cut off by the output limit), accepts `maxTokens`, and without `workspaceFiles` or `tools` is a **plain single model call**, with `systemPrompt: ''` allowed. It is metered (`usage.begin('assistant', caller)`) and passes the budget gate like every call, and its body limit is 24 MiB (`IMAGE_MAX_REQUEST_BYTES`). True. **So the vision step can use today's chat route; no platform change is needed.** Its usage kind is `assistant`, not `extract` (the milestone said `extract`: corrected here; the extraction chunks that follow are `extract`).
- The **caller** (features, AI subject) is read from the same trusted headers on every route. The service forwards `X-Authenticated-AI-Subject` today on the `extract` call only (`ai-client.mjs`); this sprint forwards it on the chat call too.
- The price table (`price-table.ts`, version `2026-11-19`): `anthropic` / `claude-sonnet-5-5` = **$2 input, $10 output per million tokens**, an image allowance of 1,600 tokens. It is not exposed over HTTP, so the service holds a **dated copy** for its estimate (a drift risk, said below).
- The platform's own `web` nginx limits `/plugins-api/` bodies to **`client_max_body_size 1m`** and `proxy_read_timeout 120s` (`apps/web/nginx.conf.template`). **A page image of 120 to 320 kB as base64 fits once, not twelve times**: the transcription route takes **one page per request**.

**This repository**

- The plugins gateway refuses any request body over **4 KiB** (`MAX_BODY_BYTES`, `gateway.mjs`); only a service route that says so may take more. Changed here with a per-route limit, tested.
- `extractChapter({ prepared, ai, … })` (`extract.mjs`) is document-agnostic: it works on `prepareChapter(pages)`'s text and offsets. Its instructions say « one zone chapter of a French local planning regulation »; a measured variant for a lotissement is **not** part of this sprint, so the prompt is unchanged. `prepareChapter` refuses fewer than 200 non-blank characters a page on average (`no_text_layer`).
- The shared result cache and the chunk memo (`jobs.mjs`) are keyed by the document and the text and **shared by every user of the deployment**. An uploaded document uses neither.
- The extension reads a PLU's result through `serviceCall`, polls a job, shows the Rules view from `writtenStateOf`, and applies `autoApplyAllowed(item)` to decide what is applied without a click (002.46). Its manifest declares `regulations`, `document`, `ai`, `commands` at SDK 1.43.
- The Peynier règlement (pages 59 to 75 of `Annexes1-4_EP-PLU.pdf`) was transcribed on 2026-11-20 by Sonnet 5.5 ($0.323, 17 pages, 178 s) and Haiku 5.5 ($0.022). A page is 2,385 input tokens at 1,568 px. Haiku read « 15-03 » as « 16-03 » and was cut off on page 74.

## Decisions

- **DEC-1 — Vision first, Sonnet, fixed. (Revised by Addendum A, DEC-A1/DEC-A2: Sonnet stays the default and the recommended route; the person may choose a local model beside it.)** The model for the transcription **and** the extraction of an uploaded document is **Anthropic `claude-sonnet-5-5`**, the one the owner approved; the AI panel's choice does not apply. An account without the `ai-anthropic` feature gets the AI service's own `provider_not_permitted`, said plainly. A local route is offered **only if Epic 1's pre-registered rule passes** (it did, on the owner's Mac too: Addendum A).
- **DEC-2 — Stateless transcription, a private job for the extraction.** `POST v1/uploads/transcriptions` takes one page image and answers its transcript at once (the extension keeps the transcripts and can show them); `POST v1/uploads/readings` takes the pages' text (a text PDF's, or the transcripts) and starts a job whose result is **held in memory, bound to the user who started it**, and dropped 30 minutes after it ends or on `DELETE`. Images are never stored, only forwarded; page text is never written to disk, never put in `results`, never in the chunk memo.
- **DEC-3 — The estimate is a route and a pure function, and costs nothing.** `POST v1/uploads/estimate` takes `{ mode, pages, characters? }` (numbers, no document content) and answers pages, model, the **about-cost** with its breakdown (transcription, extraction) and the price-table date. It sends nothing to a model. The extension asks it for the chosen range, **shows it with the heads-up, and reads only after a confirmation that names the cost**.
- **DEC-4 — Pages read, not file size.** The limit is **80 pages read per reading** (`FR_URBAN_RULES_LIMIT_UPLOAD_PAGES`, default 80), 200 pages transcribed an hour and 6 readings an hour per user, and 900 KiB of body per request. The file's size and total page count do not count (the host kept the file).
- **DEC-5 — A truncated transcription is flagged, never silent.** A transcription whose `finishReason` is `length` is answered with `truncated: true`, the page is listed in `reading.truncatedPages`, and the extension says so and offers the page image beside it. A page whose transcript is empty is `unreadable`.
- **DEC-6 — The result says where each rule came from.** Every rule carries `source: { documentId, page }` (the id the caller gave, opaque to the service) and `fromTranscript: true` when its page was transcribed from an image, so the extension can **show the page picture beside the passage** (`project-documents`' `open` at the page): a transcription error is « verified » against its own transcript, which the code cannot see.
- **DEC-7 — To confirm, never applied.** A rule with a `source` is never applied automatically: `autoApplyAllowed` refuses it. In this sprint the rules are **listed in the Documents block of the Rules view, with their source, as to-confirm**; confirming, editing or setting aside one and feeding the buildable area is **002.31** (cut, below).
- **DEC-8 — Budget.** Every model call carries the account's AI subject (ADR-0136 Rule 1). A budget stop **keeps what was read**: transcribed pages stay with the extension; the extraction's parts read stay in the result with `stopped`, exactly as the PLU reading does (002.27). Nothing is cached after a stop.
- **DEC-9 — Where the offer appears.** The Rules view (always, when the project documents service exists and the companion is located), and the reading card's end states (done, partial, stopped, failed) as one more card action (002.23's pattern). No new card kind, no permanent card.
- **DEC-10 — Fixtures and privacy of the examples.** Committed fixtures are **synthetic**, or the Peynier **règlement pages' own text** (articles of a public règlement, no resident's name), never the PDF, a page image or a transcript of another page.

## Epic 1 — Is a free local route good enough to offer beside Sonnet? (measured first)

**Candidates (both free):** (a) the local vision model the repository already configures for LM Studio, `qwen/qwen3-vl-8b` at `http://192.168.1.172:11434/v1` (slow; one page at a time, timed; skipped and recorded if unreachable); (b) an in-process OCR, **tesseract.js** with French language data, installed in a scratch folder outside the repository (no dependency is added to the repository).

**Material:** the 17 pages 59 to 75 of the Peynier règlement, already rendered at 1,568 px, and the Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 transcripts of 2026-11-20 (the earlier run, reused: $0 spent again).

**Reference:** the Sonnet transcript is a reference, **not ground truth**. For every figure or article number on which a candidate differs from it, the page image is looked at and the right reading decided; the reference is corrected where Sonnet was wrong, and Sonnet's own error is counted against Sonnet in the report.

**Measures** (the tool is `tools/measure-transcription.mjs`, scorer `tools/measure-transcription/score.mjs`, unit-tested; report in `docs/measurements/`):

1. **Figures with a unit**: every number with a unit (`m`, `m²`/`m2`, `mètres`, `%`, `cm`, `ha`, `kg`…) in the reference appears in the candidate (spaces, `m²`/`m2`, decimal comma/point, « mètres »/« m » read as equal).
2. **Article numbers**: every `NN-NN` article number and every « Article N » of the reference appears in the candidate.
3. **Wrong figures**: figures with a unit in the candidate that are not in the checked reference (a changed digit that would be « verified » against itself).
4. **Words recovered**: the share of the reference's distinct words (4 letters or more, accents folded) found in the candidate.
5. Stamps, rotated and handwritten pages: read by eye on the pages concerned, reported not scored.
6. Time per page. 7. Cost per page.

### The decision rule (pre-registered 2026-11-21, before any candidate was scored)

A local route is **offered as a choice beside Sonnet only if, on the 17 pages, ALL hold:**

| #   | Condition                                                      | Threshold                                  | Why this number                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| G1  | figures with a unit recovered                                  | **X = 95 %**                               | The chain after the transcript already finds 94.6 % of the truth rules (002.41, 002.44). A transcript that lost 5 % of the figures would roughly double the silent omissions; at 95 % the two stack to at most about 10 %. |
| G2  | article numbers recovered                                      | **Y = 95 %**                               | An article number is what a rule cites and what the owner checks; the failure to beat is Haiku's « 15-03 » read « 16-03 ». With about 60 numbers, one miss is 98 %, two are 97 %, four are 93 %: 95 % allows two.          |
| G3  | wrong figures (in the candidate, not in the checked reference) | **at most 2 %** of the candidate's figures | A changed digit is worse than a missing one: it is « verified » against the transcript that holds it.                                                                                                                      |
| G4  | words recovered                                                | **at least 90 %**                          | The extraction reads « au moins », « mètres », article titles: a transcript with holes in its prose loses the rule's meaning even with its figures.                                                                        |
| G5  | no page that holds a reference figure is empty or cut off      | none                                       | A page lost whole is the worst case and is not averaged away.                                                                                                                                                              |

**A candidate that fails any condition is not offered.** If none passes, **the product ships Sonnet-only** and the report says which condition failed. Stamps, rotation, handwriting, time and cost are reported, never a gate (a free route is slower and costs nothing by construction). The rule judges the transcription's fidelity only; it does not decide on the owner's behalf whether a lower-fidelity free route would be wanted anyway (owner question O1).

## Epic 2 — The service routes

Document-agnostic, never importing platform packages, no model key. Everything below is metered to the account's subject.

```text
POST v1/uploads/estimate       { mode: 'text'|'image', pages, characters? }          → { estimate }          (no model call)
POST v1/uploads/transcriptions { page, image: { mediaType, data } }                  → { page, text, truncated }   (since Addendum A: 202 { jobId } and a poll)
POST v1/uploads/readings       { documentId, pages: [{ page, text, transcribed? }] } → 202 { jobId }
GET  v1/uploads/readings/{id}  → { jobId, state, progress, result?, error? }         (the owner only)
POST v1/uploads/readings/{id}/cancel
DELETE v1/uploads/readings/{id}                                                      (drops the result now)
```

- The result is the PLU result's shape (`rules`, `dropped`, `chunks`, `model`, `stopped?`, `schemaVersion`, `promptVersion`) with `source` and `fromTranscript` on each rule and a `reading` block (`mode`, `pages`, `truncatedPages`, `transcribedPages`); **it is not the shared cache's result and carries no GPU document id**.
- `ai-client.mjs` gains `chat()` (one `POST /api/ai/chat`, the images, `maxTokens` 8,192, the features and the AI subject) with the same timeout and error mapping, budget refusals included.
- The transcription prompt is the measured one (`peynier-tools/transcribe.py`): transcribe exactly, in French, keep article numbers, headings and lists in reading order, read a rotated page as a person would, `[illisible]` for what cannot be read, `[tampon]` for a stamp, no summary, translation or correction, the transcription only.
- Limits and errors in the service's README; new codes `too_many_pages` (422), `upload_unreadable` (422), `not_found` for another user's job.

## Risks to say plainly

- **A transcription error is « verified » against itself.** Mitigated by the page picture beside the passage and the to-confirm status; **not eliminated**. The measured error rate of the route is Epic 1's report and the live run's, on one document.
- **The price table is a copy.** The estimate can drift from the platform's table; it carries the table's date and says « about ».
- **The extraction prompt says « zone chapter of a PLU »** and was measured on PLU chapters only; its recall on a lotissement règlement is the live run's single data point.
- **The proxy chain's limits** (1 MiB body, 120 s) shape the routes; a page that takes the model over 100 s fails that page, not the reading. **The live run's table page (91 lots) took 88.6 s**, close to that limit: a denser table could fail, and such a failure is not retried (a second try would outlast the proxy while being paid for).
- **Traces:** the AI service's traces record the page images and the transcripts like any other call, so anyone with trace access can see them. This is operator awareness, not a blocker, and nothing here changes it.
- **One document measured live.** The Peynier règlement is 17 pages of a 2009 text; nothing is claimed for other layouts.

## Addendum A — Both routes: Sonnet and a local model (owner decision O1, 2026-11-21)

> **Status:** acceptance fixed 2026-11-21 **before** any code of this addendum; the build and its evidence are under « Addendum A — Outcome » at the end.
> **Versions:** `fr-urban-rules` **0.27.0** (SDK 1.44 unchanged), `fr-urban-rules-service` 0.7.0 (still unreleased), the plugins gateway unchanged. The platform is read, not changed.

### Why

Epic 1's route question was put to the owner as **O1**. Two facts arrived the same day. **(a)** On the owner's Mac (M5 Pro, 24 GB, LM Studio at `localhost:1234`) `qwen/qwen3-vl-8b` read the 17 pages in **13.0 minutes** and **passes the pre-registered rule** (G1 98.6 %, G2 99.5 %, G3 0.5 %, G4 95.0 %, G5 none; pages 67 and 69 looped on « [illisible] » to the 4,096-token cap, 140 s each, no figure on them); the same model on the slow host (192.168.1.172, no GPU) took 3 h 39 min: **the same model's time depends on the machine, ten to twenty times**. `paddleocr-vl-1.6` (an OCR model) read the 17 pages in 60 s but **fails the rule** (figures with units 30.5 %, wrong figures 12.3 %): not offered. **(b)** **O1 is resolved by the owner: offer BOTH routes**, Sonnet (recommended, the default) and a local model, « as we do with import ». Traces: an uploaded document's calls are traced like every other call (no opt-out). The cache isolation of transcribed text stays (no shared cache).

### Decisions

- **DEC-A1 — The choice is the AI panel's, read through the mechanism that exists.** Plan import reads the model the person chose in the AI panel (default from Preferences › AI Models, ADR-0132 Rule 7) through `visionTargetFor`; an **extension** is given the same selection **as a name**, `ai.selectedModel()` → `{ providerId, model? }` (SDK 1.40, ADR-0028 Rule 11, used by the PLU reading since 002.16): never an address or a token. This addendum reuses it and invents nothing: the extension shows the **local route only when the panel's selection is a local provider** (`lmstudio`, `ollama`), names that model, and sends `ai: { providerId, model? }` exactly as `POST v1/extractions` does (here it chooses the **transcription's** model: DEC-A2). Sonnet is the preselected route whatever the panel says (it is the recommended one and the only one measured end to end); a panel that selected OpenAI or another cloud model offers no local route and reads with Sonnet as before.
- **DEC-A2 — The service accepts exactly two routes; the extraction is always Sonnet's (HYBRID, owner decision 2026-11-21 on OA1, « as per your recommendation »).** `ai` absent, or `anthropic` + `claude-sonnet-5-5` (or no model): **cloud**. `lmstudio` or `ollama` with an optional model: **local**. Anything else (a cloud provider other than Anthropic, another Anthropic model, an unknown id, an extra field) is `route_not_offered` (422). **The route is the TRANSCRIPTION's:** a local route transcribes the scans on the local model (free: the platform never gates a call to a `free` provider, so the transcription never reaches the AI budget) and **the extraction of the rules always runs on Sonnet 5.5, for every route** (text, about $0.08 for 17 pages, metered as `extract`, gated by the budget like any). Reason (measured, Addendum A Outcome): the local extraction took 685 s for one 3,400-character page and read no rule, where Sonnet read 14 rules from 17 pages in 15 s. (An earlier draft of this addendum made both steps local, from the owner's «never charges the AI budget»; the live check refuted it and the owner chose the hybrid.) A reading whose range holds no scan has nothing to transcribe locally: its cost is the extraction's.
- **DEC-A3 — Estimate.** Cloud unchanged. Local: `route: 'local'`, **transcription `$0`** (`breakdown.transcriptionMicrousd` 0; its time is not known in advance) **plus the extraction on Sonnet** (`breakdown.extractionMicrousd`, the same figure as the cloud estimate's), so `microusd`, `aboutUsd` (and its `lowUsd`/`highUsd` range) are **the extraction's** and the person sees one total about-cost. `compare` is what an all-Sonnet reading would cost (`aboutUsd`, `perPageUsd`, the savings are the difference). `timing`: Sonnet's measured **13 s a page**; the local transcription **unknown in advance**, measured **46 s a page on a recent laptop with a GPU and about 13 minutes a page on a CPU-only host**. The panel says « transcription free, time depends on your machine; about $0.0X of your AI budget for the extraction ».
- **DEC-A4 — A page is an asynchronous job.** `POST v1/uploads/transcriptions` answers `202 { jobId }`; `GET …/{id}` gives `{ state, progress: { elapsedMs, timeoutMs }, result?, error? }`; `POST …/{id}/cancel`; `DELETE …/{id}`. Private like the reading job (in memory, bound to the user, 30 minutes, no disk, no log of a page). **No call outlives a proxy any more**, for Sonnet too. A per-page timeout (cloud 180 s; local 15 minutes, `FR_URBAN_RULES_LOCAL_PAGE_TIMEOUT_MS`), cancel (aborts the call in flight), progress. A local model runs **one page at a time per deployment** (a lane), a cloud route up to three.
- **DEC-A5 — The loop guard.** A pure function judges a reply: the share of the output that is a **repeated run** (an n-gram of 1 to 8 words repeated at least 6 times in a row, or 12 for one word) is its repetition ratio. **Degenerate** when the ratio is at least **0.5**, or at least **0.2 when the reply hit the output cap**. A degenerate page is **never handed on**: it is `unreadable: { reason: 'repetition' }`, listed in `reading.unreadablePages`, left out of the extraction. A reply that hit the cap **without** repetition is `truncated`, as for Sonnet. **Limit, said plainly: through the AI service's plain `chat` route (not streamed) the guard cannot stop a page while it is generated**; it judges the finished reply, and the cost of a loop is bounded by the local output cap (**4,096 tokens**, the measured one: 140 s on the Mac) and the page timeout. Stopping early needs the platform change proposed in the report.
- **DEC-A6 — The figures notice and the cost, together.** Whenever pages were transcribed by a local model the person is told, **before the confirmation and again on the result**, that a local model's digits are not flagged when wrong (measured: 750 read 760, 308 read 200, article 15-03 read 16-03, never flagged), that the page picture stays beside every passage (« Voir la page ») and that the rules are to confirm. **The confirmation names both**: the figures check and the about-cost of the extraction on the AI budget (a local reading is not free: only its transcription is).
- **DEC-A7 — Sonnet as a one-click retry.** For each page the local model could not read (`unreadablePages`) the result offers « Lire la page N avec Sonnet 5.5 », with a confirmation that names its about-cost; the page is transcribed and extracted on the cloud route, its rules are **merged** into the kept reading, and the notice for that page goes. A second failure keeps the page listed.
- **DEC-A9 — Local pages in the project.** `reading.route` (where the pages were transcribed), `unreadablePages` and `retriedPages` are kept with the reading; the figures notice is for the pages a local model transcribed and Sonnet did not retry.
- **DEC-A8 — A local page that fails does not fail the reading.** A local page that cannot be read (loop, empty, timeout) is unreadable and the reading goes on; two failed pages in a row stop the reading with the cause (a server that is down is not waited for 17 times).

**How the choice reaches the service (and its limit).** The extension reads `ai.selectedModel()` (SDK 1.40, ADR-0028 Rule 11): a **name**, `{ providerId, model? }`, the AI panel's selection or the Preferences default (ADR-0132 Rule 7), computed by the platform's `selectedModelFor` from the same rule plan import uses (`visionTargetFor`), **without** its endpoint, connection id or token. ### Platform proposals (NOT built, no platform code: for the owner to take or leave)

1. **Tell an extension whether the selected server is its own.** A server the person added in Preferences › AI Models (ADR-0132 Rules 5 and 6: its own address and token) is selected under the provider type (`lmstudio`), so `ai.selectedModel()` cannot tell it from the operator's server and the AI service resolves `lmstudio` to the **operator's**, never the person's. Proposed: add `connection?: 'managed' | 'user'` (or `own?: boolean`) to `SelectedAiModel` in SDK 1.45, so the extension can say « your own server is not reachable from the companion service » and offer Sonnet only.
2. **Stop a looping page early.** The `chat` route is not streamed, so a loop runs to its output cap (140 s on the Mac, 35 minutes on a CPU-only host). Proposed: a streamed `POST /api/ai/chat` the client can abort (the guard's `repetitionRatio` accepts any prefix), or an optional `stop: string[]` forwarded to the provider.
